Understanding Your Speed Test Results

Internet performance depends on four critical network variables:

  • Download Speed (Mbps): How rapidly data travels from the internet to your device. Essential for watching YouTube/Netflix, downloading files, and browsing.
  • Upload Speed (Mbps): How quickly you can send data out. Crucial for Zoom/Google Meet video calls, uploading photos, and cloud backups.
  • Ping / Latency (ms): The round-trip reaction time of your connection. Lower is always better (under 30ms is ideal for gaming).
  • Jitter (ms): The variation in latency over time. Low jitter ensures voice calls don't stutter or freeze.
