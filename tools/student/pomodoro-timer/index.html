About the Pomodoro Study & Focus Timer

The XESTUS Pomodoro Study & Focus Timer is a time management utility based on the scientifically proven Pomodoro Technique developed by Francesco Cirillo. By breaking work into structured 25-minute intervals of deep focus separated by 5-minute restorative breaks, the Pomodoro Technique combats procrastination, prevents mental burnout, and helps you achieve sustained focus for studying, coding, and writing.

The Classical Pomodoro Technique Workflow
1. Focus Interval (1 Pomodoro): 25 Minutes of uninterrupted, single-task focus.
2. Short Break: 5 Minutes to rest, hydrate, and stretch.
3. Repeat 4 Cycles: Complete 4 Pomodoro focus intervals.
4. Long Break: Take a 15 to 30 Minute break to recharge before beginning the next set.

Worked Example: 2-Hour Study Session

  • 00:00 - 00:25 -> Pomodoro 1 (Deep Work)
  • 00:25 - 00:30 -> 5-min Short Break
  • 00:30 - 00:55 -> Pomodoro 2 (Deep Work)
  • 00:55 - 01:00 -> 5-min Short Break
  • 01:00 - 01:25 -> Pomodoro 3 (Deep Work)
  • 01:25 - 01:30 -> 5-min Short Break
  • 01:30 - 01:55 -> Pomodoro 4 (Deep Work)
  • 01:55 - 02:15 -> 20-min Long Rest Break
