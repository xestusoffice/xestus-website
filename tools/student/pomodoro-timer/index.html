The Pomodoro Technique Workflow

  1. Choose a task: Pick a single assignment or study topic.
  2. Work for 25 minutes: Stay completely focused with zero multitasking.
  3. Take a 5-minute break: Relax, stretch, and grab water.
  4. Repeat 4 times: After 4 sessions, enjoy a longer 15–30 minute break.
