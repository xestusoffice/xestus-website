About the CGPA to Percentage & GPA Calculator

The XESTUS CGPA to Percentage & GPA Calculator is an academic grade computation utility built for students across high schools, engineering colleges, and universities. It supports standard university credit-weighted GPA calculations (SGPA/CGPA) and provides instant conversion to percentage marks based on standard formulas (such as the CBSE 9.5 multiplier and AICTE 10-point scale formula) for job applications, scholarship forms, and higher education admissions.

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

Standard Academic Grade Formulas
1. Weighted CGPA Formula:
   CGPA = Sum(Credit_i × GradePoint_i) / Sum(Credit_i)

2. CBSE 10-Point Scale to Percentage:
   Percentage (%) = CGPA × 9.5

3. AICTE / Technical University Standard Conversion:
   Percentage (%) = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10

Worked Example: CGPA Conversion

A student with an 8.4 CGPA on a 10-point scale:
  • CBSE Standard: 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.80%
  • AICTE Standard: (8.4 - 0.75) × 10 = 76.50%
