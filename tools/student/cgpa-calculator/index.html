How to Use the CGPA to Percentage & GPA Calculator

To convert CGPA to percentage for CBSE and most Indian universities, multiply your CGPA by 9.5:

Percentage (%) = CGPA × 9.5

For VTU: Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10. For Mumbai University: Percentage = 7.1 × CGPA + 11.
