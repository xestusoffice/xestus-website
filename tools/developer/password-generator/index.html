About the Strong Password Generator & Entropy Checker

The XESTUS Strong Password Generator produces cryptographically random, high-entropy passwords to protect your accounts against brute-force attacks, dictionary attacks, and credential stuffing. Using the browser's hardware-backed window.crypto.getRandomValues() cryptographic pseudo-random number generator (CSPRNG), this tool avoids the predictability flaws of standard random functions. It also calculates mathematical password entropy in bits in compliance with NIST SP 800-63B guidelines.

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

Password Entropy & Brute-Force Resistance Formula
Password Information Entropy E = L × log2(R)
Where:
- L = Password Length (number of characters)
- R = Character Pool Size:
  * Lowercase only: R = 26
  * Lowercase + Uppercase: R = 52
  * Alphanumeric: R = 62
  * Full Symbols: R = 94
Entropy Classification:
- < 40 bits: Weak / Vulnerable
- 40 - 64 bits: Moderate
- 65 - 80 bits: Strong
- > 80 bits: Very Strong (Resistant to supercomputer brute-forcing)

Worked Example: Password Strength Comparison

  • 8-char simple password: pass1234 -> ~28 bits entropy (Cracked in seconds)
  • 16-char high-entropy password: k9#Xm$8Q!vP2@wL7 -> ~105 bits entropy (Billions of years to crack)
