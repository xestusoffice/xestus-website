What is a Version 4 UUID?

A Version 4 UUID is a 128-bit label generated using cryptographically strong pseudo-random numbers (RFC 4122). Out of 128 bits, 122 bits are entirely random, resulting in 2^122 ≈ 5.3 × 10^36 possible unique identifiers.

Collision Probability

The probability of finding a duplicate UUID v4 is practically zero. Even if you generated 1 billion UUIDs per second for 100 years, the probability of creating a single collision is less than 0.000000000001%.
