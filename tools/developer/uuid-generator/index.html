About the UUID / GUID v4 Generator

The XESTUS UUID Generator produces RFC 4122 compliant Version-4 Universally Unique Identifiers (UUIDs) and Microsoft Globally Unique Identifiers (GUIDs). UUID v4 generation relies on cryptographically secure pseudorandom numbers. With 122 bits of pure entropy, the probability of generating a duplicate UUID is so infinitesimal ($1 ext{ in } 2^{122}$) that collisions are practically impossible across distributed systems.

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

RFC 4122 UUID Version 4 Structure
UUID Format: 8-4-4-4-12 (32 Hex Characters + 4 Hyphens = 36 Chars)
Pattern: xxxxxxxx-xxxx-4xxx-yxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx
Where:
- '4' indicates UUID Version 4 (random)
- 'y' is one of [8, 9, a, b] (RFC 4122 variant: (bits & 0x3f) | 0x80)
- 'x' represents random hexadecimal digits (0-f)
