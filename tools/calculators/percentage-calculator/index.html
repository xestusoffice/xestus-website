Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

Core Percentage Mathematical Formulas
1. What is P% of X?
   Result = (P / 100) × X

2. What percentage is X of Y?
   Percentage = (X / Y) × 100

3. Percentage Increase / Decrease from X to Y:
   Percentage Change = ((Y - X) / |X|) × 100

4. Reverse Percentage (X is P% of what number?):
   Original Number = X / (P / 100)

Worked Example: Percentage Calculations

1. Percentage of Value: What is 15% of ₹25,000?
(15 / 100) × 25,000 = ₹3,750

2. Percentage Growth: Revenue increased from $40,000 to $58,000:
((58,000 - 40,000) / 40,000) × 100 = +45.00% growth

3. Ratio to Percentage: Student scored 468 marks out of 600:
(468 / 600) × 100 = 78.00%
