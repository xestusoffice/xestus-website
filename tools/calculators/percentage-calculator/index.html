Common Percentage Formulas & Examples

1. Finding Percentage of a Value

Formula: Result = (X × Y) ÷ 100

Example: 15% of 2,500 = (15 × 2500) ÷ 100 = 375.

2. Finding What Percent X is of Y

Formula: Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: If you scored 45 marks out of 180: (45 ÷ 180) × 100 = 25%.

3. Percentage Change (Increase or Decrease)

Formula: Change % = ((New Value - Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

If positive, it represents an increase; if negative, a decrease.
