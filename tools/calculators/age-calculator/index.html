How Age Calculation Works

Age is calculated by determining the precise elapsed time between your Date of Birth and a specific reference date (defaults to today).

  • Accounts for leap years (February 29th).
  • Calculates accurate months according to exact month day counts (28, 29, 30, or 31 days).
