How GST Calculation Works in India

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a destination-based unified tax levied on manufacturing, sale, and consumption of goods and services throughout India.

1. GST Exclusive (Adding GST to Base Price)

Used when the seller quotes a price excluding taxes:

  • GST Amount: (Base Amount × GST %) ÷ 100
  • Total Invoice Amount: Base Amount + GST Amount

2. GST Inclusive (Reverse Tax Extraction)

Used when retail prices already include GST (MRP):

  • Net Base Price: (Total MRP × 100) ÷ (100 + GST %)
  • GST Amount Extracted: Total MRP - Net Base Price

3. CGST, SGST & IGST Split

  • Intra-State: When buyer and seller are in the same state, GST is divided equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST).
  • Inter-State: When transaction is across state borders, Integrated GST (IGST) is applied at the full rate.
