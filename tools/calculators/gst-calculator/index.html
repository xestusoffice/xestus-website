About the GST Calculator (India Inclusive & Exclusive)

The XESTUS Indian GST Calculator is a comprehensive financial tool designed to compute Goods and Services Tax (GST) amounts, net base prices, and gross invoice amounts with instantaneous precision. Designed for Indian business owners, chartered accountants, freelance professionals, e-commerce merchants, and consumers, this tool supports both GST Exclusive (+ Add GST) and GST Inclusive (Remove GST from MRP) modes. Under the Indian indirect taxation regime, goods and services are taxed under distinct slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%). Our calculator also provides automatic bifurcations for Intra-State transactions (CGST 50% + SGST 50%) and Inter-State transactions (IGST 100%), helping you prepare accurate tax invoices and filing records.

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

GST Calculation Formulas (Inclusive & Exclusive)
1. GST Exclusive (Adding GST to Base Price):
   GST Amount = (Base Amount × GST Rate) / 100
   Total Gross Amount = Base Amount + GST Amount

2. GST Inclusive (Extracting GST from Gross Price / MRP):
   Net Base Amount = Gross Amount / (1 + (GST Rate / 100))
   GST Amount = Gross Amount - Net Base Amount

3. Tax Split Breakdown:
   Intra-State: CGST = GST Amount / 2, SGST = GST Amount / 2
   Inter-State: IGST = GST Amount

Indian GST Tax Slabs & Rate Chart (2026 Reference)

Under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) system in India, items and services are categorized into 5 primary tax rate slabs:

GST Slab Tax Split (Intra-State) Eligible Goods & Services Examples
0% (Nil GST) CGST 0% + SGST 0% Fresh vegetables, milk, curd, unbranded wheat/rice, salt, fresh fruits, eggs.
5% Slab CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5% Packaged food items, edible oil, tea, coffee, life-saving medicines, railways, economy air travel.
12% Slab CGST 6% + SGST 6% Business computers, processed foods, butter, cheese, ghee, mobile phones, diagnostic test kits.
18% Slab (Standard) CGST 9% + SGST 9% IT & software consulting, telecom, restaurants, financial services, hair oils, consumer electronics.
28% Slab CGST 14% + SGST 14% Luxury motor vehicles, air conditioners, refrigerators, aerated drinks, high-end consumer goods.
Special Rates Custom / Split 3% on Gold, Silver, and Jewellery; 0.25% or 1.5% on rough or cut diamonds.
