How to Use the Loan EMI Calculator

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is the fixed monthly amount a borrower pays back to a lender to repay a loan. The formula used is:

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]
  • P: Principal Loan Amount
  • R: Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • N: Loan Tenure in Total Months
