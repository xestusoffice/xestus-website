How to Calculate Date Differences Accurately

Calculating the number of days between two dates is essential for project timelines, lease agreements, interest calculations, visa validity periods, and event countdowns. This tool accounts for leap years, variable month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days), and separates working business days from weekend days.

About the Date Difference & Duration Calculator

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

Date Interval Computation Logic
Total Milliseconds = EndDate.getTime() - StartDate.getTime()
Total Days = floor(Total Milliseconds / (1000 × 60 × 60 × 24))
Total Weeks = floor(Total Days / 7) + (Total Days % 7) Days
Calendar Months & Years = Chronological date subtraction accounting for Gregorian leap rules
