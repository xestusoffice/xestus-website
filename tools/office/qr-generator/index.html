QR Code Generator (Custom & Downloadable)

Create custom QR codes for URLs, WiFi, plain text, UPI payments, and phone numbers with custom color palette and PNG download.

About the Custom QR Code Generator

The XESTUS Custom QR Code Generator creates high-resolution, scannable Quick Response (QR) codes in PNG and vector SVG formats according to the ISO/IEC 18004 standard. Generate custom QR codes for website links, UPI payment handles, Wi-Fi login credentials, vCard contact cards, email messages, and plain text. With selectable Reed-Solomon error correction levels (Low, Medium, Quartile, High), your QR codes remain scannable even if partially damaged or printed on textured surfaces.

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

QR Code Error Correction Standards (ISO/IEC 18004)
Reed-Solomon Error Correction Levels:
- Level L (Low): Recovers ~7% of damaged codewords (Highest data capacity)
- Level M (Medium): Recovers ~15% of damaged data (Standard for web URLs)
- Level Q (Quartile): Recovers ~25% of damaged data (Ideal for industrial labels)
- Level H (High): Recovers ~30% of damaged data (Best for codes with embedded center logos)

Worked Example: Wi-Fi QR Code Format

Payload: WIFI:S:MyHomeWiFi;T:WPA;P:SecretPassword123;;
Scanning this QR code on any smartphone instantly connects the device to the Wi-Fi network without manual password entry.
