Why Accurate Character Counting Matters

From SEO meta title limits (approx. 60 characters) and description lengths (155-160 characters) to SMS limits (160 characters per segment) and social media publishing, maintaining exact character budgets prevents truncation and improves engagement.

About the Character & Letter Counter

The XESTUS Character & Letter Counter provides real-time character analytics for writers, social media managers, and digital marketers. It computes total characters with spaces, characters without spaces, word count, sentence count, paragraph count, and estimated reading/speaking time while tracking real-time limits for Twitter / X (280 chars), Meta Title (60 chars), Meta Description (160 chars), and SMS (160 chars).

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

Text Metrics & Reading Time Estimation
1. Characters (No Spaces) = Text.replace(/\s+/g, '').length
2. Word Count = Tokens matching /\b\w+\b/g
3. Reading Time = Word Count / 200 words per minute (WPM)
4. Speaking Time = Word Count / 130 words per minute (WPM)
