Text Case Converter

Transform text formatting instantly across all naming conventions: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Capitalized Words, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case.

Case Conventions Explained

  • UPPERCASE: ALL CHARACTERS IN CAPITAL LETTERS (e.g. HEADINGS, CONSTANTS).
  • lowercase: all characters in small letters.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes The First Letter Of Every Major Word.
  • Sentence case: Capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence.
  • camelCase: firstWordLowerNextWordsCapitalized (used in JavaScript/Java variables).
  • snake_case: words_separated_by_underscores (used in Python).
  • kebab-case: words-separated-by-hyphens (used in URLs and CSS classes).
