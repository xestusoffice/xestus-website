About the Word & Paragraph Counter

The XESTUS Word & Paragraph Counter is an editorial writing companion for authors, journalists, students, and SEO content strategists. It delivers real-time word counts, sentence counts, paragraph metrics, estimated reading time, speaking duration, and automated keyword frequency analysis to optimize content length for essays, academic papers, and blog posts.

Mathematical Formulas & Technical Mechanics

Under the hood, calculations and operations strictly adhere to validated standards:

Text Analytics Formulas
Word Count = Tokens matching non-whitespace word boundaries (\S+)
Reading Time (Minutes) = Word Count / 200 WPM
Speaking Time (Minutes) = Word Count / 130 WPM
Keyword Density (%) = (Keyword Occurrences / Total Words) × 100

Worked Example: Essay Analysis

A 1,000-word article:
  • Estimated Reading Time: ~5.0 Minutes
  • Estimated Speaking Time: ~7.7 Minutes
  • Optimal paragraph count: 6-8 paragraphs
