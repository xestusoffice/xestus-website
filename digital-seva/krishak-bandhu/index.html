How to Check Krishak Bandhu Status with Voter ID (ধাপে ধাপে পদ্ধতি)

1

Visit the Official Statutory Website

Open your web browser and navigate directly to krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in. Ensure you are on the genuine .wb.gov.in official portal.

2

Click on "নথিভুক্ত কৃষকের তথ্য" (Registered Farmer Info)

On the homepage menu, locate and click the green tile labeled "নথিভুক্ত কৃষকের তথ্য" (Search Voter Card / Registered Farmer Information).

3

Select Identification Type & Enter Voter ID (EPIC)

From the dropdown list, select "Voter Card" (or Aadhaar/KB ID if available). Enter your 10-character Voter ID Number (e.g. WB/xx/xxx/xxxxxx or ABC1234567).

4

Solve the Security reCAPTCHA & Submit

Tick the box that says "I'm not a robot" (complete image verification if prompted) and click the green "Search" button.

5

Inspect Your KB ID & Payment Transaction Status

Your record will appear on screen showing: KB ID, Farmer Name, District, Block, Gram Panchayat, Total Land in Acre, and Transaction Status (e.g., Transaction Successful).

Krishak Bandhu Payment Status Explainer (স্ট্যাটাসের অর্থ কী?)

When searching your status on the portal, you will see specific administrative remarks. Here is the official meaning of each status:

Status Text Stage Meaning & What You Should Do
KB ID Generated Registration Your application is accepted and a unique Krishak Bandhu Identification Number has been assigned by the Department of Agriculture.
Approved Verification Your land records (Banglarbhumi Porcha) and KYC documents have been successfully verified and approved by the Block ADA office.
Uploaded Bank Batching Your payment file has been batched and sent to the State Treasury / PFMS for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) credit into your bank account.
Account Valid Pre-Disbursement The bank has validated your Account Number, Aadhaar seeding, and IFSC code. The money will be disbursed shortly.
Transaction Successful Completed ✅ The installment money (₹5,000 or ₹2,000) has been successfully credited directly to your bank account! Check your passbook or SMS.
Transaction Failed / Invalid Account Attention Needed ⚠️ Bank account number mismatch, dormant account, or missing Aadhaar NPCI seeding. Visit your Block ADA office immediately with your passbook to update.

Krishak Bandhu Death Benefit Scheme (কৃষক বন্ধু মৃত্যুজনিত অনুদান — ₹২,০০,০০০)

Under the Krishak Bandhu Death Benefit component, if a registered farmer or recorded sharecropper (Bhagchasi) between the age of 18 and 60 years passes away, the legal nominee or family receives a one-time grant of ₹2 Lakh (Two Lakh Rupees) to ensure family livelihood security.

Documents Required for Claim:

  • Original Death Certificate issued by Panchayat / Municipality
  • Deceased farmer's Krishak Bandhu Card / KB ID / Porcha
  • Age proof (Admit Card, Voter ID, Aadhaar) showing age between 18–60 yrs
  • Legal Heir Certificate / Nominee Declaration from BDO/SDR
  • Nominee's Bank Passbook & Aadhaar Card

Where to Submit Claim:

Submit the completed Krishak Bandhu Death Benefit Claim Form at the local Block Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) office or BDO office within 30 days of demise.

Frequently Asked Questions (সাধারণ প্রশ্নোত্তর)

Visit the official portal krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in, click on "নথিভুক্ত কৃষকের তথ্য", select 'Voter Card' from the dropdown, type your 10-digit EPIC number, solve the captcha, and click Search. Your KB ID, land area, and installment credit status will appear immediately.
The Government of West Bengal releases Krishak Bandhu funds in two installments:
  1. Kharif Installment: Disbursed around June–July ahead of sowing season (up to ₹5,000).
  2. Rabi Installment: Disbursed around December–January for winter cultivation (up to ₹5,000).
New applications are submitted in person with your Banglarbhumi RoR (Porcha), Aadhaar Card, Voter Card, and Single Bank Account Passbook at your nearest Duare Sarkar camp or the Block Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) office. Online direct registration for new farmers is processed through the department's authorized portal.
For any portal or payment query, contact the official Krishak Bandhu helpline numbers: 8336957370 / 8597857373 or landline 033-2214-1378. You can also email the department directly at krishak.bandhu@yahoo.com.
